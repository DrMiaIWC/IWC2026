The Purpose of the Core Kit :

The Core Kit was made to help give people a way to help themselfs in case of emergency and they can't reach a hospital in time, or in other cases some people get anxious and have panic attacks when they need to go to a hospital. For example imagine there is a flood and somehow you have a allergic reaction, have the Core Kit with a epipen in it will help you until you can get to a hospital. Another example is , somebody may have a deep wound that they need professinaly wrapped but they may have panic attacks when they think of hospital. The Core Kit is here to help prevent that from happening.

The Small Details of the Core Kit :

The Core Kit is here to help for a small price of $445, included in the Core Kit is the Epipen , Inhaler , Alcohol Pads , Band Aids , and a Covid 19 Vaccine . The Core Kit can be found in retail stores and pharmacies near you . For example, your local Walmart , Target , CVS and Walgreens.
